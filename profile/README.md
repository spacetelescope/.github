[Space Telescope Science Institute Website](https://www.stsci.edu/)

We are the Space Telescope Science Institute in Baltimore, Maryland, operated by the Association of Universities for Research in Astronomy. We help humanity explore the universe with advanced space telescopes and ever-growing data archives.

**Our Core Values**:
- **Service**: We serve the science community and the public in their endeavors to explore and understand the universe.
- **Integrity**: We commit to fostering an environment of honesty, respect, trust, and ethical behavior.
- **Legacy**: We build on our achievements by laying the foundations for the next generation of astronomy space missions.
- **Excellence**: We enable the extraordinary in our missions by leveraging the skills of our uniquely talented staff.
